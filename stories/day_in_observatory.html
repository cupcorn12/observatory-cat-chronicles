A day in the Observatory

Welcome to the story of "A day in the Observatory"

So the first meeting of the day starts at 8am. The Agents and cats all huddle together except for a few people. They talk about the mission of exploring a cave system in a planet and the Agents that do the mission. The Agents on the mission are Leo, Aries Capricorn and surprisingly Atlas. Cosmic cat prepares a horoscope for everyone.

Everyone goes to Taurus' cafeteria at 9am for breakfast and the people on the mission talk about the mission. At 9:30am everyone splits to their groups. Admiral Tuna and Healer doing their duties, Cosmic cat and Scorpio being mysterious, Gemini, Virgo and Quill archiving the day, Sagittarius preparing the map room and Aquarius, Pisces and Cancer making a flashlight that flashbangs bats.

The mission lasts a few days so Capricorn brought camping supplies and Taurus baked lots of food.

1:30pm: The creators lose track of time and miss lunch at 1pm but Taurus always comes to the groups and brings round lunch.

Taurus: "it's like a delivery service."

At 2pm scorpio decides he wants to scare the Archivists and Sagittarius gets dragged in.

2:30pm, scorpio: "BOO!"

The Archivists gasp in shock.

Sagittarius: "Umm... I'm here too."

Virgo: "Aren't you supposed to be preparing the map room."

Sagittarius: "yeah, but I've finished. It doesn't take long."

Quill and Gemini slowly look at Scorpio with a sour look.

Scorpio: "hehe... I was only trying to scare you guys."

The Archivists go back to work.

Scorpio:"Well, that didn't work."

Sagittarius: "can I go now?"

Scorpio: *deep sigh* "yeah, I guess..."

Sagittarius:"Yay!"

Sagittarius goes to Leo's book nook and Scorpio thinks about how to scare the Creators

At 6pm there's a daily meeting of how the day went. Taurus also made dinner. Gemini brings the Creators at 6:30 and Sagittarius arrives at 6:15

8pm: Everyone is dismissed and almost everyone goes to their rooms except for the Archivists and Admiral Tuna. Gemini secretly archives Scorpio's scare

10pm: Cancer is on night watch because Leo isn't here and Admiral Tuna does the day guard.

3 days later around 3pm the mission group comes back exhausted and dirty.

Leo:"Aries caused us to loose an afternoon."

Aries:"There was a pool."

Capricorn:"well we did find some cool minerals."

Atlas:"aaand i got lost."

They clean themselves and relax until the debriefing at 6pm. They talk about what they found in the cave system. The Explorers archive the cave system for future use.
