Admiral Tuna: he/him

Admiral Tuna is the protector and warrior of the Observatory. His trusty companion is Agent Leo, because she once said she liked tuna sandwiches. This is what the Warriors are about, after all.

Admiral Tuna's room is an armoury that every Agent visits before going on a mission. Leo also goes there to train with him, and she's good at it.

Admiral Tuna is always alert and never attends meetings because he is the day guard, while Leo is the night guard.

Another thing to note is that Admiral Tuna failed to protect the Observatory in the past. Perhaps he is training Leo to be a better warrior, or perhaps he is training himself to become stronger so he doesn't fail again.
