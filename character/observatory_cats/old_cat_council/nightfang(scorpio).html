Nightfang: she/her

Nightfang was one of the reasons the Old Cat Council left. She managed to interpret one of Cosmic Cat's predictions, becoming the only one to decipher a prediction before the event happened.

The prediction was about Admiral Tuna being too weak to defend the Observatory and failing. Nightfang immediately called a meeting and told everyone. Cosmic Cat and Admiral Tuna were especially angry.

The prediction came true, despite everyone hoping it wouldn't. Everyone felt that the Observatory was no longer safe, so they all left.

Cosmic Cat hid for a while, while Admiral Tuna tried to cheer everyone up. Eventually, they formed the Agents, hoping that the Agents wouldn't decipher their predictions.
