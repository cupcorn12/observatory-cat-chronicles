Static: she/her

When the Old Cat Council left, Static wanted somewhere to relax, so she searched everywhere for a vacation planet. She eventually managed to get a job there.

Static's spaceship is full of ideas inspired by her imagination, and she uses those ideas to create unusual attractions for visitors.

Static likes it there because she doesn't have any rules to abide by, although she occasionally visits the Observatory.
