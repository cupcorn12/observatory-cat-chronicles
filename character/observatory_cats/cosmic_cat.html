Cosmic Cat: they/them

Cosmic Cat is as old as the universe. Because of this, they are the founder and CEO of the Observatory.

Cosmic Cat is the one who gives the cryptic horoscopes and predictions during their meetings.

Cosmic Cat's room is full of their own predictions, except for one window where they always sit and contemplate. Leo was pretty happy about the window because Cosmic Cat usually dislikes it when anyone does something like that. However, Cosmic Cat clearly liked it.

Cosmic Cat and Scorpio always seem to end up together, although no one knows why.

Cosmic Cat: "The timeline of the Observatory is this: me, the Old Cat Council and Admiral Tuna, then the Agents, then Healer, and finally Quill and Atlas."

Gemini: "What was that about the Old Cat Council?"

Cosmic Cat: "What's the Old Cat Council?"

Gemini: *sigh* "You keep doing that."

Despite that dialogue, Cosmic Cat cares about the Old Cat Council. Whenever one of them is in trouble, Cosmic Cat calls a meeting as soon as possible.
