Quill: she/her

Quill is part of the Archivists and spends a lot of time in the library, mainly because she joined to be a librarian.

Quill's room is an office next to the library and Gemini's room. As a result, Gemini and Quill are often together, except when Gemini is with Leo and Aries.

Gemini was originally the main Librarian, but she was getting swamped and needed more time, so Quill joined and ended up becoming the main Librarian. Gemini was a little sad about that, but didn't really mind.
