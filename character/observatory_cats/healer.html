Healer: she/her

Healer is the medic of the Observatory and has a proper medbay and surgery room.

Healer joined because a mission ended really badly and the Agents needed a medic. Luckily, Healer was on the planet they visited.

Aries was part of that mission and couldn't walk for a while, so Healer tried to stop him from exploring. It actually worked.

On a separate occasion, Leo got a paperclip from a project the Creators were working on, and Healer patched her up.

Healer feels inadequate because she's one of the newest members of the Observatory Cats, although seeing Atlas and Quill join helped a little.
