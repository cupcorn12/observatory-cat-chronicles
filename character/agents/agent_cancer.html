Agent Cancer

Welcome to the Agent Cancer book

Agent Cancer: he/him, Cherub: she/her

Agent Cancer has a cat named Cherub, and she secretly goes through the Observatory looking for people who are unhappy.

Agent Cancer is a therapist and a hermit. He doesn't like to leave his room, although Cherub often makes him leave to comfort people.

Agent Cancer's room is styled like a therapy room and has cosy bean bags and stuffed animals, although Agent Cancer doesn't like people in his room.

Agent Cancer doesn't like the Explorers because being part of the group means leaving his room and possibly the Observatory. However, he likes the Explorers because there's a lot of emotional issues in that group.
