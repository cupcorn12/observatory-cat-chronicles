Welcome to the Agent Aquarius book

Agent Aquarius: he/him, Leviathan: she/her

Agent Aquarius has a cat named Leviathan. Leviathan helps write down the crazy ideas Aquarius thinks up. Leviathan doesn't like her name, but considering the crazy ideas he has thought of, she is surprised he didn't pick a worse one.

Agent Aquarius is an inventor and has made all sorts of crazy designs that usually don't work.

Agent Aquarius' room is a library full of his ideas, but most of them go into the "Too Crazy to Work" section, so that section fills up about three walls.

Although Aquarius often makes projects that don't work, he occasionally creates something that makes the other Agents' lives easier.
