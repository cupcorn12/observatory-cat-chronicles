Agent Scorpio: he/him, Bearer: she/her

Agent Scorpio's cat is called Bearer. She disappears for most of the day, except when Scorpio hides something. In that case, she tells everyone the secret he was keeping.

Agent Scorpio is a keeper of secrets and knows everyone's weaknesses because Leo made pathways in Scorpio's room that lead to everyone's rooms.

Agent Scorpio's room is a secret. No one knows what it looks like except for Leo, which means he can disappear into his room whenever he wants.

Agent Scorpio has a mysterious relationship with Cosmic Cat, and no one knows how they got so close. It's actually because he discovered the secret that Nightfang revealed but kept hidden.
