Agent Libra: she/her, Lion: he/him

Agent Libra has a cat called Lion, and he yowls whenever there's a fight going on. It doesn't matter where he is; he just knows.

Agent Libra is a peacemaker and usually breaks up fights within the Main Three.

Agent Libra's secret room is a timeout area where people can go to get away from it all. It is hidden behind Leo's secret book nook.

The Main Three are exempt from punishment because they fight far more often than anyone else, and Libra got sick of putting them in her room multiple times a day.
