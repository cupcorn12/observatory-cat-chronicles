Welcome to the Agent Pisces book

Agent Pisces: she/her, Spot: she/her

Agent Pisces has a cat named Spot, and she always ends up with paint splatters on her, even when she's cleaning the art supplies.

Agent Pisces is a dreamer and an artist. Leo often comes to her to see whether her ideas are any good.

Agent Pisces' secret room is full of easels, sketchbooks and all the supplies needed to use them.

Agent Pisces sells her paintings to bring in money for the Observatory, even though it runs on some source of ancient power.
